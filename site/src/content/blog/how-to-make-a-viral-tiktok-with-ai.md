Most viral short-form videos aren't lucky accidents. They reuse a format people already love, hook the viewer in the first second, and they're posted while the trend is still climbing. AI video tools take care of the slowest part, which is the editing, so you can spend your time on the idea.

Here's the workflow we use at FAYAA.

## 1. Start from a format that already works

Don't start with a blank timeline. Start with a format that's already proven:

- **Trend dances.** The moves are set, so you only have to show up in them.
- **Snap transitions.** One outfit, a snap, then a new look in a new place.
- **POV runs.** A first-person camera through a place people want to see.
- **Product hooks.** A product drop, slam or reveal in the first three seconds.
- **Street interviews with a twist.** It looks like a normal interview until it suddenly isn't.

When you start from one of these, viewers already know how to watch it, so they keep watching.

## 2. Make the first second impossible to scroll past

Viewers decide in about a second. Open on motion, not a title card. A product hitting the table, a camera rushing forward or a snap mid-gesture all work better than a slow fade in.

If you use an AI prompt, name that moment: *"a 3-second hook where the sneaker slams into frame."*

## 3. Put yourself (or your product) in the shot

This is where AI earns its keep. With motion transfer, you upload one clear photo and the AI recreates the source clip's movement, camera and timing with you in it. With a product swap, your product replaces the one in the clip, while the hands and lighting stay the same.

Tips for clean results:

- Use a sharp, well-lit photo, facing the camera.
- Add more reference images to lock the face and outfit. FAYAA accepts up to four.
- Keep source clips short and steady while you're learning.

## 4. Make three versions, not one

Small changes can make a big difference. Make the same video three times with a different hook, setting or style, like a neon street, a rooftop or an anime restyle. Then post the one that feels strongest, or test them all against each other.

Because each version takes seconds, testing costs you almost nothing.

## 5. Post vertical, post fast

Export in 9:16 at 1080p, add your caption and sound in the app, and post while the trend is still rising. Being early to a trend matters more than having a perfect edit.

## The short version

Pick a proven format, hook in the first second, put yourself in the shot with AI, make a few versions, and post quickly. That loop is how creators publish every day without spending every evening editing.
